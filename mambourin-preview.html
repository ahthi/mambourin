What's On

Welcome to Mambourin Marketplace

Opened in September 2025, Mambourin Marketplace is a vibrant new shopping centre serving the rapidly growing communities of Mambourin and surrounding suburbs in Melbourne's west.

Anchored by a full-line Coles supermarket, our centre brings together fresh food markets, popular dining options, health and beauty services, fitness, and a wide range of specialty stores — all in one convenient location at 36 Delama Boulevard.
